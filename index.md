# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

**Brand Name:** Stephen Menke (or your registered Sole Proprietor Brand name)

This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and shared when you interact with our personal notification system and SMS messaging services.

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us when setting up or opting in to receive communications. This includes:

* Your mobile phone number.
* Any verbal or explicit consent details provided for receiving family-related notifications.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly to:

* Deliver automated family alerts, system status notifications, or personal project updates via SMS.
* Respond to your requests or inquiries regarding the notification service.

### 3. Sharing of Information

> **We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.**

Information is never shared with third parties, affiliates, or partners for promotional or commercial campaigns. Data sharing is strictly limited to essential routing with telecommunications carriers required to deliver the text messages.

### 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

### 5. Opting Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by texting **STOP** to the sender number. Once you send "STOP," we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to_time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

### 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly via your standard personal communication channels with the system administrator.
